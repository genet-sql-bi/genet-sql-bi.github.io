City Care Hospital Analytics

An end-to-end healthcare analytics project: MySQL, SQL data-quality checks and analysis, and a 3-page Power BI report that answers the questions a hospital operations director asks every month. Where do patients wait? Are doctors used where demand is? Where does the money come from, and what is still unpaid?

My data-quality checks showed the original sample couldn't support department- or hospital-level analysis (for example, 165 of 200 appointments were at a different hospital from their doctor). I fixed the keys and rebuilt the dataset; it now passes every key and reconciliation check with 0 mismatches.

What the checks found

03 / DATA QUALITY

The project started from a 100-patient sample. Before building any visuals, I ran 10 checks (coverage, category values, admission logic, billing reconciliation, ranges, duplicates, cross-table consistency, overlapping stays). The sample failed badly:

CheckMismatches in the original sample
Appointment hospital vs its doctor's hospital165 of 200
Admission department vs its doctor's department75 of 80
Admission hospital vs its department's hospital60 of 80
Bill patient vs its admission's patient79 of 80
Tables covering different periodsLabs ended in April, schedules in February

How I fixed it

04 / VALIDATION
  • Source-of-truth rule set from the organization: Department → Hospital, Doctor → Department, Encounter → Doctor, Bill → Admission
  • Keys repaired, then the data regenerated with those rules enforced, scaled to 1,000 patients over 12 months
  • 0 mismatches now on every key and reconciliation check; every bill reconciles to the cent
  • 13 overlapping stays flagged (check 10) and excluded from the readmission analysis
  • Load bugs fixed: Windows line endings silently breaking text matches, and blanks loading as 0 instead of NULL

The 1,000-patient dataset was generated with an AI-assisted Python script to my specification, using published clinical and payer norms. Every assumption is listed in the script's CONFIG section.

Recommendations

06 / ACTIONS
Access

Lengthen follow-up slots

Follow-ups wait twice as long as they're seen. Resizing these slots shortens the queue without adding staff.

Access

Remind follow-up patients

Follow-ups no-show at 9.7% vs 6.4%. Targeted reminders recover booked slots that are currently lost.

Staffing

Rebalance clinic capacity

Shift spare sessions from low-use departments like Dermatology toward Orthopedics and General Medicine.

Inpatient

Plan beds around three diagnoses

Fracture, Covid and Cancer drive 60% of bed days, so bed forecasting should start with them.

Quality

Strengthen chronic discharge follow-up

Cancer, Diabetes and Hypertension lead 30-day readmissions. Post-discharge follow-up is the clearest lever.

Cash

Earlier self-pay payment plans

45% of self-pay revenue is pending. Payment plans at admission would reduce the unpaid balance.
